# 2026-11-01 — Shallot and Guanciale Tart

Weekend project. Adapted from Joshua McFadden, *Six Seasons* (Onion and Pancetta Tart, pp. 352–353, with the Pecan Dough on p. 49 made with walnuts).

## Swaps
- Guanciale for pancetta
- Shallots for yellow onion
- Cowgirl Creamery Wagon Wheel (pressed, washed-rind cow's milk) for Gruyère

## What went wrong
- Dough made the day before, chilled overnight, rolled cold. It cracked and felt soft in places.
- Lined pan chilled only 5 minutes before baking.
- After 10 minutes of blind baking with weights, the walls had slumped and the base had domed.
- Cause (moderate confidence): warm butter at the oven door. Weights were not the cause. Whether the weights were packed against the walls is unknown.

## Outcome
Family loved it. The daughter went back for seconds and asked for it as her birthday dinner.

## Next time (untested)
- Rest dough 15–20 min until it bends before rolling.
- Ease it into the pan, do not stretch. Leave dough above the rim.
- Freeze the lined pan 20 min.
- Pack weights against the walls, filled to the rim.
- Press the base flat with a spoon if it domes.
