# 2026-11-11 — Gyudon

Weeknight. Cookbook gyudon (dashi, shoyu, sake, mirin, sugar, onion, ginger). Made with frozen shaved rib eye (shabu-shabu slices), cooked straight from frozen as the recipe's notes allow.

## What went wrong
- The sauce filled with gray, sandy grit: coagulated meat protein (*aku*).
- Cause (high confidence): the beef went in frozen and thawed in the sauce below a simmer. Freezing had torn the muscle fibers, so the slices leaked extra juice. The juice set slowly as fine grains instead of clumping into foam. Breaking up the frozen block with a spoon crumbled the grains further.
- ¾ cup of liquid in a wide nonstick skillet was too shallow to skim. The protein dried onto the bare pan.

## Outcome
Taste and family reaction not recorded.

## Next time (untested)
- Thaw the beef fully: sealed bag in cold water, 15–20 min. Pat dry and pull apart.
- Blanch (*shimofuri*) in batches, 10–15 sec in water just short of a boil. Drain.
- Simmer the sauce and onion in a small saucepan, not a skillet.
- Give the beef 1–2 min in the sauce, not 5. Shaved rib eye toughens fast.
- If you skip the blanch: double the liquid, add the beef in two batches at a steady simmer, skim with a fine-mesh strainer.
