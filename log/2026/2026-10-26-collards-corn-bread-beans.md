# Sunday Collards, Crispy Corn Bread, and Oven Beans

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Cooked:** Sunday Collards and Crispy Corn Bread (both Lee Bros., published on the site), plus Kevin's own pot of beans.

## Kevin's beans (as told, not yet measured)

1. Sauté shallots in bacon fat.
2. Strip the collards. Mince the ribs small and add them to the pot.
3. Mince celery small. Add it when the ribs are done.
4. Do the same with carrots.
5. Add garlic.
6. Add 2 dried chiles, about 1 tsp Rancho Gordo bean seasoning, and 2 California bay leaves.
7. Add the soaked beans and water to about 1 inch above the beans.
8. Add 2 dried shiitake and 2 pieces of dried seaweed (kombu).
9. Bring almost to a boil. Move to a 300°F oven.
10. Pull the seaweed after 30 minutes. Cook until tender. The pot never really boils.

## Patterns worth noting

- The collard ribs go into the bean base, so the whole bunch gets used. The leaves go to the collards and the ribs go to the beans.
- Shiitake and kombu build an umami base with no meat beyond the bacon fat. Pulling the kombu at 30 minutes keeps it from turning slimy or bitter.
- A 300°F oven holds the pot below a boil. The beans stay whole and the skins stay intact.
