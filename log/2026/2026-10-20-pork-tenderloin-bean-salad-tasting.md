# Tasting Notes: Fennel-Rosemary Pork Tenderloin + Anchovy Bean Salad

**Feedback date:** 2026-10-20
**Dishes:** Fennel-Rosemary Pork Tenderloin, Insalata di Fagioli con Alici e Erbe (Judith Barrett, *Fagioli*)
**Served:** sliced pork laid over the bean salad

## Verdict

The pairing did not work. Kevin's partner said the two dishes competed to be the star. The beans had a strong flavor. The pork had a strong flavor. They did not support each other.

## Why it missed

Both dishes were built to lead. The beans carry anchovy, garlic, red wine vinegar, and raw parsley. The pork carries toasted fennel, rosemary, thyme, and chili. The plan was contrast: a warm, resinous meat against a salty, acidic salad. In practice the two loud profiles clashed instead of contrasting. Laying the pork on the beans made it worse, because no bite tasted of only one dish.

## Lesson

Pair a bold dish with a quiet one. Pick one star per plate.
- The anchovy bean salad wants a plain partner: grilled bread or a simply roasted chicken.
- The fennel-rosemary pork wants a plain side: white beans in olive oil, roast potatoes, or bitter greens with lemon.
