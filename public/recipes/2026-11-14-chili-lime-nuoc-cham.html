Chili-Lime Nuoc Cham

2026-11-14 · Makes about ½ cup

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 2 Tbsp warm water
  • 3 Tbsp lime juice
  • 1 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • 1 small fresh chile, thinly sliced

Method

  1. Stir the sugar into the warm water until it dissolves.
  2. Add the lime juice, fish sauce, garlic, and chile.
  3. Let it sit at least 10 minutes before serving.

Notes

It is great with crab.

Dissolve the sugar first. It dissolves slowly in cold lime juice and leaves grit. Warm water handles it in seconds.

This version leans sour and salty, with less sugar than most. The 10-minute rest lets the garlic and chile flavor the whole bowl. Use a Thai bird chile for real heat or a Fresno for a milder one.
