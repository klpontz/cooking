Instant Pot Mexican Rice

2026-11-14 · Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 1 can (10 oz) diced tomatoes with green chiles
  • 2 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1½ cups long-grain rice, rinsed and dried
  • 1¼ cups chicken stock
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano

Method

  1. Purée the tomatoes and chiles, with their juice, and the garlic in a blender until smooth.
  2. Heat the oil in the Instant Pot on Sauté. Add the rice and stir until it smells nutty and the grains start to turn translucent, about 3 to 4 minutes.
  3. Add the chicken stock and the purée, then the salt, cumin, and oregano. Stir, and scrape the bottom of the pot so no rice sticks.
  4. Lock the lid and cook on high pressure for 9 minutes.
  5. Let the pressure release naturally. Fluff the rice, mix, and serve.

Notes

Rinse the rice to wash off loose starch, then let it dry well. Wet rice steams in the oil instead of toasting. Toasting gives the nutty flavor and helps the grains stay separate.

Puréeing the tomatoes and garlic spreads the flavor through every grain. Chunks would sit on top. The purée is thick, so scrape the bottom well before you lock the lid. Rice stuck to the pot can set off the burn warning.

The natural release finishes cooking the rice in its own steam. If you release early, the grains on top stay firm.

It goes well with the Chile Verde Pork.
