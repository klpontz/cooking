Ponzu-Butter Dip

2026-11-14 · Makes about ⅓ cup

Ingredients

  • 4 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 Tbsp ponzu, plus more to taste
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 small garlic clove, smashed, or 1 thin slice ginger

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a small saucepan over low heat.
  2. Stir in the ponzu, rice vinegar, and soy sauce.
  3. Add the garlic or ginger and let it steep over the lowest heat for 3 minutes.
  4. Take out the garlic or ginger.
  5. Taste. Add more ponzu for brightness or more butter for richness. Serve warm.

Notes

It is great with crab.

Melted butter and ponzu do not stay mixed. Stir before each dip, or whisk it in a warm bowl at the table.

Steeping and removing the garlic or ginger gives a clean hint of it without raw bite. Smash the garlic so it is easy to fish out. If you grate it, strain the dip instead.

For a sharper dip with no butter, try the Chili-Lime Nuoc Cham.
