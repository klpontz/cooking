Weeknight Coconut Chicken Curry

2026-07-03 · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • Neutral oil
  • 3 Tbsp Thai red curry paste (Maesri or Mae Ploy)
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 cup chicken stock (or water)
  • 1.5 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 1 lime (juice of half, plus wedges to serve)
  • To finish: fresh basil or cilantro, sliced fresh chili, steamed jasmine rice

Method

  1. Bloom the paste. Heat 1 Tbsp oil in a wide skillet or Dutch oven over medium. Add the curry paste and fry 1–2 min until it darkens and smells toasty. Add garlic and ginger, 30 seconds more.
  2. Split the coconut milk. Spoon the thick cream from the top of the can into the pan and let it sizzle and split with the paste, 2–3 min, until the fat separates.
  3. Brown the chicken. Push the paste aside, add the thighs, and let them pick up color, 3–4 min. They don't need to cook through yet.
  4. Simmer. Add the rest of the coconut milk, stock, fish sauce, and sugar. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook 10 min. Add bell pepper and onion, simmer another 5–7 min until the chicken is done and the peppers are tender-crisp.
  5. Balance and finish. Off heat, add the lime juice and taste. It should hit salty-savory first, then a whisper of sweet, then bright. Adjust: flat → more fish sauce; too rich → more lime; want heat → sliced fresh chili. Top with basil and serve over jasmine rice with lime wedges.

Notes

Coconut curries fall flat when they skew sweet. The fixes are structural: blooming the paste in oil until it darkens develops the aromatics rather than leaving them raw and one-note, and splitting the coconut cream over that toasted paste builds a fried, savory base before any liquid goes in. The balance comes at the end — fish sauce anchors the salt-umami floor, only a teaspoon of sugar rounds the edges, and lime off the heat keeps the brightness alive instead of cooking it away. Curry paste (3 Tbsp) lands mild-medium; keep chili on the side so it works for the whole family. Sub firm tofu (pan-crisped first) or shrimp (added in the last 4 min) for the chicken, or swap green curry paste and Thai basil for a fresher version.
