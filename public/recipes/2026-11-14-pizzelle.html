Pizzelle

2026-11-14 · Makes about 20

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • ¾ cup sugar (white or cane)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp anise extract
  • 8 Tbsp (1 stick) butter, melted and cooled
  • 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1¼ tsp anise seed
  • Cooking oil spray or melted butter, for the iron

Method

  1. Turn on the pizzelle iron and let it heat up.
  2. Whisk the eggs and sugar until the mixture turns pale yellow and falls off the whisk in thick ribbons.
  3. Whisk in the vanilla extract, anise extract, and melted butter until combined.
  4. Fold in the flour with a spoon or spatula in 3 or 4 batches. Add the anise seed with the last batch. Stir only until no dry flour shows.
  5. Spray the iron with oil or brush it with butter. Do this once at the start. You do not need to repeat it.
  6. Cook about 1 Tbsp of batter per cookie, following the instructions for your iron. It goes fast, about 30 to 45 seconds.
  7. Lift each pizzelle out with a wooden spatula and cool it on a rack. They turn crisp in 1 to 2 minutes.

Notes

There is no baking powder. Whipping the eggs and sugar to the ribbon stage gives the batter its lift. Without baking powder the cookies bake thin and snap clean instead of turning cakey.

Cool the butter before it goes in. Hot butter can start to cook the eggs and knock the air out of them.

Pizzelle are soft when they come off the iron and crisp as they cool. If you want to shape them into cones or bowls, do it in the first few seconds. Store them airtight once they are fully cool.

Lemon pizzelle: leave out the anise extract and anise seed. Use 2 tsp vanilla extract in total. Add 1¼ tsp lemon zest with the extract and butter.
