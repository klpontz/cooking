Sunomono

2025-05-13 · Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice wine vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • ⅔ cup sugar
  • 1 Tbsp kosher salt
  • 4 cups Japanese or Persian cucumbers, thinly sliced

Method

  1. Combine the vinegar, water, sugar, and salt in a saucepan and bring to a boil, stirring until the sugar and salt dissolve.
  2. Take the pan off the heat. Add the cucumbers.
  3. Lay a towel or paper towel over the top and push it down into the liquid so the cucumbers stay under.
  4. Let cool to room temperature, then chill. Serve cold.

Notes

Adapted from J. Kenji López-Alt. His version uses ¾ cup sugar. This one uses ⅔ cup so it reads tart before sweet.

The hot brine softens the cucumber skins and lets the slices take on flavor fast, while the centers stay crunchy. The towel keeps floating slices under the liquid so they pickle evenly.

Japanese and Persian cucumbers have thin skins and small seeds, so there is no peeling or seeding. Slice them about ⅛ inch thick.
