Asian Slaw

2019-08-17 · Serves 6 to 8

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup soy sauce
  • ¼ cup packed brown sugar
  • ¼ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 Tbsp rice vinegar, plus more to taste
  • 1 Tbsp Chinese black vinegar
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 20 turns black pepper
  • 2 small heads cabbage, thinly shredded
  • Carrots, shredded (about 1 part carrot to 3 parts cabbage)
  • Green onions, white parts only, thinly sliced
  • Cilantro, roughly chopped
  • Roasted peanuts, chopped
  • Toasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Whisk the soy sauce, brown sugar, vegetable oil, sesame oil, rice vinegar, black vinegar, salt, and pepper in a bowl until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Put the cabbage, carrots, green onion whites, and cilantro in a large bowl. Use the ratios in the notes as a starting point.
  3. Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss well. Let the slaw sit 15 minutes so the cabbage softens a little.
  4. Taste. Add a splash more rice vinegar and more salt if it needs it.
  5. Top with the peanuts and sesame seeds just before serving so they stay crunchy.

Notes

The dressing makes enough for 2 small heads of cabbage, about 12 cups shredded. The vegetables are up to you. These ratios are starting points, by volume of shredded or chopped vegetables:

  • Cabbage to carrot: about 3 to 1. Go to 4 to 1 if you want the slaw to stay pale and crisp, or 2 to 1 if you want it sweeter.
  • Cilantro: about 1 loose cup per head of cabbage.
  • Green onion whites: 4 to 6 per head.
  • Peanuts: about ¼ cup per head. Sesame seeds: about 1 Tbsp per head.

The black vinegar gives a dark, malty depth that rice vinegar alone does not have. The brown sugar balances the soy and the two vinegars. Taste after the rest, not before. The cabbage gives off water and dilutes the dressing, so it usually needs more vinegar and salt at the end.

The original version also called for lemon and ginger. They were left out. Add a squeeze of lemon or some grated ginger if you want more brightness or heat.
